FoodFlow Studio

feed data → it checks, learns, forecasts

1Feed it data

Give it a food-price file (CSV or Excel) with at least: a date, a market/place, a food name and a price. Extra files (weather, fuel, exchange rates) with a month column become extra clues automatically.

2Train

It studies the past, then sits 4 fair exams: train on the past only, predict the next 6 months, repeat. It must beat the lazy guess "same as last month".

Leave foods unselected to use all with enough history.

3Forecast

Next month's price per food per market, with a low-high range (the honest "we are not sure" part) and an up/down/steady arrow.

download CSV

4Ask the analyst

Answers come only from the trained results, never invented. Plug in a free open-source LLM (Ollama) on your PC for free-form chat; without it, a built-in rule brain answers.
